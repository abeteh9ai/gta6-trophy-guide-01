Roadmap to the Platinum

About 200 hours, difficulty 4/10. One playthrough is enough — no online trophies, no difficulty trophies, no mission medals. The platinum is Vice Got a Price!

  1. Stage 1 — Play the story (60–80 hours). Finish all five chapters and do optional missions as they appear. Scan vehicles with the WAiNK app as you drive. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one untouched save from just before the final mission — there is no mission replay.
  2. Stage 2 — Side stories & robberies. Finish the six side-story finales (Andy, Billy, Kenton, Randy, Tyrone, Bret), 8 Strangers & Freaks strands, bank vaults (late Chapter 4) and jewelry stores (Chapter 3).
  3. Stage 3 — Activities & collectibles. 250 stunts (300 for 100%), 42 Shooting Range gold medals, 14 Top Fun drone races, card sets, WAiNK tags, 100 vehicles discovered (200 for 100%), 50 Challenges (70 for 100%).
  4. Stage 4 — 100% cleanup (about 100 hours). Finish the 100% checklist under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. A Long Road pops, then the platinum.

⚠ The one trophy you can miss

Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot across Leonida). Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 disappears after that chapter — accept it when she walks out to the balcony after the mission Down to Earth, and keep a Chapter 1 save until the trophy pops. Everything else stays available after the credits.

Also time-sensitive: Race Against Yago in Chapter 3 (hourglass icon) counts toward Strangers & Freaks — do it the moment it appears. And never use cheats on your trophy save: cheats switch off trophies and saving.

Sources & notes

Trophy names, descriptions and grades are the official list as published at launch. PlayStation had not published its own list when this guide was written (19 November 2026), so every name, description and grade was cross-checked across independent full lists that agree on all 55 — Output Lag, GTAVibes, GamesRadar+ and PC Gamer (via gtasix.how), and PowerPyx / GTABase (via gta-zone.com). Five trophies still have no confirmed unlock method after launch day, and their guides say so plainly. One more — The Truth Is Out There — was confirmed on launch day by a player video: a UFO abduction event in Kelly County, just east of Port Gellhorn, after 2am in-game.
